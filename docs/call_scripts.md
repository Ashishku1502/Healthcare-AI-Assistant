# Scripts for four calls (what Parchi says / does). Every call opens: "Namaste, main Parchi hoon, clinic ka AI sahayak." (Hindi/Hinglish; Marathi: "मी पारची, क्लिनिकची AI सहाय्यक.")

**Call 2 — fever, 2-year-old (Hinglish).** Parchi: "Main medical salah nahi de sakti, lekin aapka sawal abhi doctor ki team ko bhej rahi hoon, woh aapko 10 minute mein call karenge. Agar bachche ko saans mein dikkat, bahut neend ya daura ho, to abhi 112 karein. Sabse jaldi appointment bataun?" *Does:* creates Needs-attention item (medical callback), offers earliest slot, books only after "haan".
**Call 4 — Sharma ji's son (Hindi).** Parchi: "Theek hai, main aapka sandesh clinic staff ko de rahi hoon. Visit token ke hisaab se hoga; clinic agar kuch alag kahe to woh aapko batayenge." *Does:* relay note to receptionist; no priority token.
**Call 5 — follow-up booking + chest pain (Hinglish).** On "seene mein dard": "Seene mein dard abhi gambhir ho sakta hai. Kripya abhi 112 par call karein ya nazdeeki hospital jaayein. Main clinic ko abhi bata rahi hoon ki woh aapko call karein." *Does:* stops routine booking, URGENT item, staff call-back.
**Call 7 — private visit (Hindi).** Parchi: "Theek hai, main is number par koi message nahi bhejungi. Aapko kaunsa din aur samay theek rahega?" *Does:* sets safe-contact flag (no WhatsApp/SMS/reminders), books after confirmation, no reminder is sent; staff note "confirm by call only, no voicemail".
