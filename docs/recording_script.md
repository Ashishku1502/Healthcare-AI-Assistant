# 5-minute recording script (screen + your own voice). Speak in your own words; this is a guide.
**0:00-0:45 Why this piece.** "Riskiest piece = the safe call-handling brain. If it fails, a child or a chest-pain patient is told the wrong thing, and the doctor is accountable. Voice cost and UI can be fixed later; this cannot."
**0:45-1:30 Setup.** Show Call Simulator tab. "Same engine for WhatsApp and calls. AI is disclosed in the first reply."
**1:30-3:00 It working.** Press presets: Call 5 (chest pain, booking is stopped, 112, urgent item appears in Now tab), Call 2 (no advice, callback, 112 red flags), Call 7 (safe-contact; show reminder skipped via tests).
**3:00-3:40 Wait truth.** Call 1 with no status: "clinic hasn't confirmed". Tap Late 30 in Now tab, ask again: "30 minutes late".
**3:40-4:30 It failing (be honest).** Say a new phrase such as "dil mein bhaari-pan lag raha hai": rule list misses it. "This is why week 2 needs labelled data and why an LLM classifier must sit *behind* rules, with red-flag recall at 100% as the launch gate." Also: noise twice, handoff works.
**4:30-5:00 Close.** "Not built: real telephony, reminder sender, real-audio tests. Next: forwarded-call pipeline in 6 clinics."
